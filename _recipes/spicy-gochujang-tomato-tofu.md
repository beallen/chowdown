ingredients:
- 1/4 cups and 1 tablespoon of long grain rice
- 1 tablespoon and 1 teaspoon of vegetable oil
- 1 packaged of firm tofu (15 ounces) cut into 1cm cubes.
- 1/3 cup gochujang chili paste (adjust for heat)
- 4 cloves of garlic
- 2 and 1/2 tablespoons brown sugar
- 4 and 2/3 cups of cherry tomatoes, halved
- Sesame seeds (garnish)
- Green onions (garnish)
- Crushed peanuts (garnish)


directions:
- Cook rice according to normal instructions.

- Meanwhile, add the oil to a saute pan over medium-high heat and cook the tofu until golden on all sides, about 10 minutes.

- Meanwhile, mix the gochujang, garlic, soy sauce, and sugar in a bowl. Set aside.

- When the tofu is golden, transfer it to a plate. Add the tomatoes to the now empty pan, and cook for 3 minutes.

- Then, add the sauce and cook for an additional 3 minutes.

- Stir in the tofu, then serve alongside the warm rice. Garnish as desired, and enjoy!
