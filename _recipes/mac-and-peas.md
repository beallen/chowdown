ingredients:
- 8 ounces of dried pasta (e.g., macaroni or fuscilli)
- 1 and 1/4 cups frozen peas
- 100 grams frozen spinach (optional)

components:
- All-purpose cheese sauce

directions:
- Prepare the all-purpose cheese sauce.

- Bring a large pot of water to a boil for the pasta. Cook the pasta according to the package directions. Drain.

- Return the pasta to the pot and add the peas and the cheese sauce. Stir and heat over low until heated throughout and slightly thickened. You can also add the peas to the pasta during the last 30 seconds of cooking.
