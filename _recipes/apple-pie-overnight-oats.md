ingredients:
- 1 cup steel cut oats
- 1 and 1/2 cups unsweetened soy milk
- 1 tablespoon pure maple syrup
- 1/2 to 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/2 teaspoon vanilla
- pinch of salt
- 1 large apple (grated)
- 3 tablespoons chia seeds
- Allspice (to taste)
- Ground ginger (to taste)
- Additional nuts, fruit, or nut butter as toppings

directions:
- In a medium bowl, combine all ingredients except for the toppings and mix.

- Cover and refrigerate the oats overnight to allow for thickening. 

- Portion oats into bowls and add your favorite topping. Additional soy milk can be added to adjust the final thickeness of your oats.
