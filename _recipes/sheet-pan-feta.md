ingredients:
- 3 cups cooked chickpeas
- 2 pints (16-20 ounces) cherry tomatoes
- 1 shallot
- 0.25 cup extra virgin olive oil
- 2 tbsp honey
- salt to taste
- 1 teaspoon mild chili flakes
- 2 (6-8 ounce) blocks of feta, sliced 1 inch thick


directions:
- Heat the oven to 400 degrees. On a sheet pan, stir together the chickpeas, tomatoes, shallot, olive oil, honey, and chili flakes. Season with salt, then spread in an even layer. Arrange the feta among the chickpeas.

- Roast until the feta and tomatoes are soft and the chickpeas are golden brown, 30-35 minutes (no need to stir). Eat right away.
